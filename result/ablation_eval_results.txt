=== Ablation Evaluation Results ===

Model: Longformer-110M
---------------------------------
No Attack Accuracy: 99.72%
Synonym-only Accuracy: 97.85%
Paraphrase-only Accuracy: 95.92%
Logic-insertion Accuracy: 96.88%
Three-strategy Combined Accuracy: 94.62%

Model: BERT-base
---------------------------------
No Attack Accuracy: 99.06%
Synonym-only Accuracy: 97.42%
Paraphrase-only Accuracy: 92.11%
Logic-insertion Accuracy: 95.05%
Three-strategy Combined Accuracy: 91.72%

Summary:
- Paraphrasing causes the largest accuracy drop for both models.
- Logic insertion causes moderate performance degradation.
- Synonym/character replacement has the weakest effect.
- The combined attack shows the strongest degrading effect due to complementary perturbations.
