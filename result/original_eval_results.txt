=== Evaluation on Clean (Original) Test Set ===

Model: Longformer-110M (fine-tuned)
Accuracy: 99.73%

Model: Qwen-Turbo (zero-shot classification)
Accuracy: 73.65%

Model: BERT-base
Accuracy: 70.18%

Model: DistilBERT
Accuracy: 65.11%

Summary:
The fine-tuned Longformer-110M achieves the highest performance on the clean dataset due to its ability to handle long-range dependencies in multi-turn fraud dialogues. 
In contrast, general LLMs (Qwen-Turbo) and traditional Transformers (BERT-based models) show significantly lower performance, primarily due to token length limits and lack of task-specific optimization.
